Immediate dispatch on right-swipe
When ON, right-swiping a job in Discover fires the auto-apply queue NOW instead of waiting up to 5 minutes for the next cron tick. Default OFF.

{# Plan 78 § D.5 (0.4.0.20) — dry-run toggle. When ON, cron stamps `submission_artifacts.dry_run_at` instead of dispatching to the ATS. #}
Dry-run mode
When ON, the cron records what would have submitted but never calls the ATS. Use to tune the score threshold safely. Default OFF.
{# Plan 78 § D.4 (0.4.0.14) — global drain queue button. Flips every queued DRAFT back to SAVED in one shot. CSRF gated by the form. #}
Drain auto-apply queue
Move every queued DRAFT back to SAVED. Doesn't delete any drafts; just stops the cron from submitting them.
