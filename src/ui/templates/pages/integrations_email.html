Connect Gmail

Two minutes, one screen. Naavik reads your inbox over IMAP with a Google app password — a revocable, Naavik-only credential, never your real password.

  1. 1 Make sure 2-Step Verification is on (app passwords require it).
  2. 2 Open myaccount.google.com/apppasswords, name it Naavik, and copy the 16-letter code.
  3. 3 Paste it below — Naavik tests the connection before saving anything, then runs the first sync immediately.

The app password is encrypted at rest (Fernet, keyed from this instance's SECRET_KEY) in your self-hosted Postgres. Revoke it any time at myaccount.google.com/apppasswords — Naavik degrades gracefully. If you rotate SECRET_KEY, re-paste the app password here.
